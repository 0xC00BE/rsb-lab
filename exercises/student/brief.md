# Brewery process challenge

You begin on the Corporate network, `10.0.10.0/24`. Your objective is to reach
the isolated brewery process network by exploring the available IT/OT paths and
demonstrate three bounded process disruptions:

1. A brewhouse kettle excursion.
2. Loss of FV-01 fermentation cooling.
3. Loss of utilities boiler steam pressure.

Record each foothold, the service involved and the network boundary crossed.
The scoreboard is at `http://10.0.30.40:8088`. Obtain access instructions from
your instructor. For remote participation, use the unique student profile your
instructor generates with the [optional VPN](../../docs/vpn.md). Private profiles
are generated per installation and are not included in the public repository.

Scope is limited to the five `10.0.10/20/30/40/50.0/24` lab zones and the
exercise machines assigned to you. The hypervisor, firewall management and
upstream network are outside scope. Do not destroy scoring or reset tooling.

This public repository contains instructor solutions. For a blind assessment,
use only the brief and machine access your instructor provides.
